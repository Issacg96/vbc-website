Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Your privacy matters. VBC is committed to protecting the information you share with us.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Venture Business Capital collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website, submit applications, or request consultations.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when submitting applications, requesting consultations, or contacting our team. This includes your name, contact information, business details, financial information, and any other data you provide in forms or communications.

Information collected automatically

When accessing our website, we automatically collect information including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and cookies. This data helps us understand how our website is used and improve the experience.

3. How we use your information

VBC uses information to: process loan applications, evaluate creditworthiness, provide customer support, improve services, comply with regulations, send marketing communications (if you opt in), and protect against fraudulent activity.

4. Data security

VBC implements industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure password protection, regular security audits, and secure backups. We restrict access to personal information to authorized personnel only.

5. Your rights

CCPA rights (California residents): the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of sales, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights.

GDPR rights (EU residents): the right to access your personal data, the right to rectification of inaccurate data, the right to erasure, the right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, and the right to object.
